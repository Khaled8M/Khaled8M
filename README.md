## 🌱 Featured Project

### AGRI-EYE TRIO K
**Hybrid Geospatial Agricultural Decision-Support Platform**

AGRI-EYE TRIO K brings together geospatial analysis and agricultural workflows in a desktop application with online and offline capabilities.

Key areas of development include:

- **GIS & Field Mapping** — Working with farm boundaries and geospatial data.
- **Satellite Remote Sensing** — Agricultural analysis workflows using Google Earth Engine and satellite imagery.
- **Vegetation & Moisture Indicators** — Exploring indicators such as NDVI and other crop-monitoring metrics.
- **Offline Geospatial Analysis** — Working with local raster data and supported GeoTIFF imagery.
- **Drone Inspection Planning** — Developing workflows to identify candidate areas for closer field inspection.
- **Reports & Data Export** — Generating reports and exporting supported geospatial formats.

The platform is designed to help users review agricultural indicators, identify areas that may need closer inspection, and organize follow-up fieldwork.

Indicators are decision-support tools and do not independently confirm crop diseases, water quantities, or expected yields. Results depend on the available data, processing methods, and field conditions.
